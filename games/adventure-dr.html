HOW TO PLAY
⚔ QUEST
Make choices to navigate the story. Each decision shapes your path.

🃏 CARD COMBAT
You start with 3 energy per turn. Play cards from your hand — each card costs energy. Draw more cards as needed. End your turn to let the enemy act.

🛡 DEFENCE
Shield absorbs damage but resets each turn. Stack it early.

⚜ REWARDS
After battles, choose new cards to add to your deck. Build your perfect build!

🏕 CHECKPOINTS
Rest sites save your progress and restore some HP.

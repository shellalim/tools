Text Extraction Utility

This tool allows you to extract specific parts of text dynamically from one or multiple lines. It is designed to help process structured strings such as database fields, logs, or system-generated text.

You can extract values based on: scope characters (e.g. ( ), [ ]), specific keywords, or special characters. The extraction supports first, last, or N-th occurrences.

Proposed Protection Rules
1 Item yang Sudah Masuk MTS

Item yang sudah terdaftar di MTS tidak diperbolehkan muncul atau diproses kembali di PTC list.

Tujuan:
Mencegah duplikasi tracking
Menjaga satu sumber kebenaran (single source of truth)

2 Ongoing PTC vs MTS Creation

Jika item masih dalam status ongoing di PTC, maka:

MTS tidak boleh dibuat, meskipun FVI sudah berstatus ACC

Tujuan:

Menjaga urutan proses yang benar

Mencegah loncat proses sebelum PTC selesai
